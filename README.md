# Duellium

Duellium is a competitive wizard dueling game built directly into Discord.

Available in 🇬🇧 English and 🇫🇷 Français, with a language setting for each server.

Create your wizard, choose your Hogwarts House, learn spells, improve your abilities, and challenge other players to ranked duels.

Your Duellium profile is global: your character, progression, spells, and stats follow you across every Discord server using Duellium.

## ✨ Main Features

- ⚔️ **Real-Time Wizard Duels**  
  Challenge other players and react to incoming spells with Protego, Expelliarmus, or Dodge.

- 🪄 **Spell Progression**  
  Unlock new spells by meeting learning requirements, passing Knowledge Trials, and training with other players.

- 📈 **Character Progression**  
  Earn XP, level up, improve your spells, and spend Talent Points on Endurance, Magic Power, Speed, and Agility.

- 🏆 **Ranked Competition**  
  Every completed normal duel counts. Wins earn points while losses can reduce your score.

- 🌍 **Local & Global Leaderboards**  
  Compete for the top position in your Discord server or across every server using Duellium.

- 🏰 **House Competition**  
  Duel victories earn points for your Hogwarts House in each server's House leaderboard.

- 🤝 **Spell Training**  
  Experienced players can help other players practice toward learning new spells.

- 💾 **Persistent Profiles**  
  Your progression is saved and stays with your Discord account across servers.

## 🎮 Getting Started

Create your character:

```text
!profile
```

Choose your House and begin developing your wizard.

Challenge another player:

```text
!duel @player
```

Useful commands include:

- `!spellbook`
- `!leaderboard`
- `!globalleaderboard`
- `!houseleaderboard`
- `!help`
